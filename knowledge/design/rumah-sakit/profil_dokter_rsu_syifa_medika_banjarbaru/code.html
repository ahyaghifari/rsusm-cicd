person Biography & Expertise

dr. Ahmad Syarif is a highly regarded Internal Medicine Specialist at RSU Syifa Medika. He completed his specialization at the University of Indonesia and has since been a pioneer in integrated patient care in the Banjarbaru region.

His clinical focus includes the treatment of diabetes mellitus, hypertension, and complex gastrointestinal disorders. He is known for his holistic approach, ensuring that every patient receives not only medical treatment but also education on lifestyle modifications to ensure long-term wellness.

Core Competencies:
